## S8 Wedge dowels

With H7/m6 the clearance is -0.012...+0.008 mm and two round holes must also absorb the pin-spacing error (+/-0.3 each at ISO 2768-m): the head will not drop onto the wedge by hand. The head's azimuth on the wedge is not accuracy-critical (encoder and pointing model take it).

Fix: hole A 4 E7 round, hole B 4 E7 x 6 slot on the pin line; spacing +/-0.05 called out. Clearance 0.008–0.028 mm; yaw play ≤ 175″; lateral ≤ 0.014 mm.

## S13 Flat gaskets

The face gap from MJF tolerance (up to 0.9 mm over 150 mm) is larger than the 0.15 mm usable compression range of a 1 mm gasket, so low spots leak. Production uses O-ring cord in a groove with a hard stop (S12), as D88 says.
