Open-source three-axis astro head

A star tracker you can open with three hex keys.

Canopus is a motorised head for cameras and small telescopes up to 7 kg. It tracks in alt-az or on its removable wedge, with strain-wave gears that have almost no play and an encoder on every axis.

What it does

Tracks the sky

Stars, the Moon or the Sun. Go-to, a seven-term pointing model and offline plate solving in the app.

Shoots the landscape

Panoramas, path-lapse and time-lapse with any camera; Holy Grail ramps, HDR and focus stacks with a Sony on USB-C.

Runs on its own

A screen, four buttons and a red night mode on the head. Jobs on the shutter port carry on with the phone asleep.

Talks to your software

ASCOM Alpaca and LX200 built into the head, over its own Wi-Fi. No PC driver to install.

Three axes, two ways to set up

Azimuth turns without a cable stop, altitude tilts from −10° to +95°, and roll turns the camera about the lens axis to frame or to cancel field rotation.

Alt-az, straight on the tripod

For landscapes, panoramas and wide-field astro. Azimuth and altitude follow the target; roll keeps the stars at the same angle through a long sequence. The wedge stays at home.

EQ, on the removable wedge

For long lenses and long exposures. The wedge tilts 0–75° (latitudes 15–90°, north or south) and the head tracks on one axis. One coin-slot screw holds it.

Five payload types, up to 7 kg

Pick the type closest to your kit and the head sets its own roll and altitude limits, so a long lens never swings into the tower or the tripod.

P1 Mirrorless with a standard zoom 1.55 kg · L-plate
P2 Mirrorless with a 70–200 f/2.8 1.95 kg · lens collar

Open, and built to be repaired

The firmware and both apps are open source. Inside, everything comes apart with the tools in the box.

  • —One hex-key set. 2, 2.5 and 3 mm keys; M3, M4 and M5 screws only.
  • —Swappable battery. A 72 Wh pack of four standard 21700 cells, out without tools.
  • —Axis cartridges. Each axis is one module held by four screws and one plug.
  • —Standard parts. Bearings, belts, connectors and cells you can buy anywhere.
  • —Open source. Firmware, Android and Windows apps, on GitHub.
