# RFQ — battery pack ST-050

**Content:** 4 × Molicel INR21700-P50B (2S2P), nickel strip spot welding, the ST-602 battery board (supplied by us from JLCPCB, or made by the assembler from `hardware/electronics/fab/st-602/`), shell ST-605/612 (MJF at 100, moulded at 1,000, D88), gauge learning cycle and an end-of-line pack test.

**Candidates:** CM Batteries (lists a 2S2P 21700 7.4 V 10 Ah pack, small batches), Shenzhen First Power (Alibaba, automatic spot welding), Aceon (UK, small runs). Links in `docs/08-production.md`.
