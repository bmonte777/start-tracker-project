Specifications

Every number, and where it stands

These are the figures for prototype P1. A figure marked calculated comes from the design maths; target is what the production head aims for. All are measured on P1 before anything is sold.

Motion and accuracy

AxesAzimuth, altitude, roll
Azimuth rangeUnlimited, no cable stop
Altitude range−10° to +95°
Roll rangeSet by the payload type (for example +6° / −172° with P1)
Slew speed8°/s on every axis, −20 to +40 °C
Tracking, EQ on the wedge≤ 3″ RMS over 5 min at 300 mm (2.2″)calculated
Tracking, alt-az≤ 5″ RMS over 5 min, altitude ≤ 60° (2.7″)calculated
Settling after a slew≤ 2 s to within 2″
Go-to accuracy≤ 2′ (2σ) after alignment; ≤ 20″ after a plate solve
Play at the output≤ 10″ with encoder correction
GearsStrain-wave reducer after a 3:1 belt, on every axis
EncodersAbsolute magnetic encoder on each axis output

Connectivity and control

WirelessWi-Fi 2.4 GHz (own network or joins yours), Bluetooth LE for set-up
SensorsGNSS position and time, tilt watch, magnetometer
Protocols in the headASCOM Alpaca, LX200, WebSocket API
Camera controlSony on USB-C to the phone or PC; any camera with a wired remote on the shutter port
On the head1.3″ OLED screen, four buttons, status light with red night mode

Mounting and set-up

ModesAlt-az on the tripod, or EQ on the removable wedge
Wedge range0–75° (latitudes 15–90°, north or south)
Latitude adjustmentAbout 55′ per turn of the screw
Camera sideArca-Swiss clamp, 60 mm
Tripod side3/8″-16 socket on the head and on the wedge
