First night, in five steps

  1. 1 Charge Any USB-C PD charger on port A, up to 45 W. The head runs while it charges.
  2. 2 Join its Wi-Fi Page 3 on the head shows the network Canopus-XXXX and its password. Join it from your phone or PC.
  3. 3 Open the app Press Look again on Connect. Outdoors the head finds its own position and time.
  4. 4 Pick your payload Choose the payload type closest to your kit and balance it, so the head knows its limits.
  5. 5 Align and go Sync on two or three stars, or plate-solve, then go to a target. Tracking starts by itself.

Updates and recovery

Update from the app's Firmware screen or the head's own web page. A failed update rolls back by itself; if the head will not start, the web flasher rewrites it over USB from Chrome or Edge.

Repair and parts

Each axis is a cartridge held by four screws and one plug, and the 2, 2.5 and 3 mm hex keys open everything. Step-by-step repair and the parts list follow the first prototype.

Questions

Do I need a router or the internet in the field?

No. The head makes its own Wi-Fi network; your phone or PC joins it. Bluetooth is only for giving the head your home Wi-Fi once, and for an emergency stop or park.

Which cameras work?

Any camera with a wired remote socket, through the head’s 2.5 mm shutter port. A Sony on USB-C to the phone or PC adds live view, camera settings, exposure ramping, HDR and focus stacks.

Does it work with my astronomy software?

The head speaks ASCOM Alpaca and LX200 over Wi-Fi itself, so software that supports either connects directly, with no driver on the PC.

Alt-az or the wedge?

Alt-az for landscapes, panoramas and wide-field astro; the roll axis keeps the stars at the same angle. Use the wedge for long lenses and long exposures.

How cold can it get?

It is designed to run from −20 to +40 °C. In the cold the pack gives less: about 7.2 hours of tracking at −20 °C by calculation.

Can I use it in the rain?

The prototype is splash-proof (IPX4); production heads are designed to take water jets (IPX6). Let it dry before you open the battery door or store it.

How much does it cost?

No price yet: the first prototype is still being built and measured.
